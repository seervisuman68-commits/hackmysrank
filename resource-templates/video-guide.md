## 2. Structure and timing
| Time | Section | What to show |

## 3. Notes
- Say what the user sees and why it matters, not only what you click.
- If the camera, mic or scoring fails during recording, say so and show a screenshot backup instead of cutting the problem out.

## 4. Recording tips
- Record at 1080p+, quiet room, clear audio.
- Hide the bookmarks bar; use a clean browser window.

## 5. Upload and share
- Export as MP4, confirm the sound works, and set link access so reviewers can open it without signing in.
